# Prompts de animación

## Objetivo

Animar las imágenes base para construir un reel de cuatro escenas sobre TDAH, ruido visual y atención.

## Escena 1 - Preocupación

Animate this image as a vertical 9:16 clip in a cute modern animated style.

Keep the same cream-and-white short-fur kitten, same desk, same silver laptop, same warm lamp lighting, same bookshelf background, same camera angle, same framing, and same kitten size in frame.

The kitten appears worried and overwhelmed, surrounded by floating notifications and visual clutter. The notifications should have subtle motion only. The kitten blinks, holds its head, and looks stressed.

## Escena 2 - Caricia / contención

Use the final frame of scene 1 as the exact starting pose.

Animate this as a vertical 9:16 clip.

The kitten starts stressed, with paws on its head and notifications around it. Then a human hand slowly enters from the side and gently pets the kitten on the head. The petting should feel calm, soft, affectionate, and reassuring.

As the kitten is petted, it gradually lowers its paws and relaxes. The notifications remain at first with minimal animation only, then feel calmer and less dominant.

## Escena 3 - Reducción de estímulos

Use the final frame of scene 2 as the exact starting pose.

Animate this as a vertical 9:16 clip.

The kitten stays calm while the hand slowly and gently moves away from its head. The air should gradually clear during the clip. The sparkles should slowly fade out and disappear little by little. The red notification should softly dim and fade away until it disappears.

Do not add any new notifications, sparkles, floating icons, or interface elements. Only fade out the existing visual clutter.

## Escena 4 - Paz final

Use the clean final image as the exact starting frame.

Animate this as a vertical 9:16 clip.

The kitten stays peaceful and becomes slightly happier in a soft natural way. Use only subtle motion: soft blinking, tiny gentle head movement, and calm breathing. Keep a serene forward gaze.

Do not add any new notifications, sparkles, floating particles, or interface elements. Keep the scene clean, stable, and quiet.
